# Midpoint Mean Reversion — Plain-English Table

Every candle has a midpoint — the price exactly halfway between its high and its low. After the 9:30 candle closes, **does price return to that midpoint?**

## Headline table

(Sample = 223 trading days)

| When did price return to the 9:30 midpoint? | Days in sample | % of days |
|---|---|---|
| Within 30 minutes after close (by 10:15) | 144 | **64.57%** |
| Within 75 minutes after close (by 11:00) | 159 | **71.30%** |
| At any point during the rest of the RTH session | 176 | **78.92%** |

**Plain reading:** ~65% of days mean-revert to the opening candle's middle within 30 minutes. By the end of the day, ~79% have touched the midpoint. **About 21% of days never return** — those are the pure trend days where the open's directional read holds all session.

## Trade implication

The midpoint is a viable target for fade trades on the extremes — about 2/3 reach it within 30 minutes. The 21% of trend days where the midpoint never gets touched are where you'd lose money fading.

## How reliable?

223 days, ~11 months. Aggregate ±3pp.

## Glossary

- **Midpoint**: the exact middle of the candle's range. For a candle with high=100 and low=80, the midpoint is 90.
- **Mean reversion**: when price returns toward the average level after extending away from it.
- **Trend day**: a day where price moves in one direction without significant pullback.
