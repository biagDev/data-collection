# When Does NQ Make Its High/Low of the Day? — Plain-English Table

For each RTH session, we record when (which hour bucket) NQ set its highest high (HOD) and lowest low (LOD).

## Headline table

(Sample = 223 trading days)

| Hour of the day | High of day was made here | Low of day was made here |
|---|---|---|
| 9:30-10:00 (the open) | **31.39%** | **34.98%** |
| 10:00-11:00 | 13.90% | 16.59% |
| 11:00-12:00 | 9.87% | 11.66% |
| 12:00-13:00 | 6.73% | 6.28% |
| 13:00-14:00 | 5.83% | 8.97% |
| 14:00-15:00 | 8.52% | 6.73% |
| 15:00-16:00 (the close) | **23.77%** | 14.80% |

**Plain reading:** Classic U-shape. **The first 30 minutes and the last hour together account for ~55% of HODs and ~50% of LODs.** Midday (11:30-2:00 PM) is the dead zone — only ~22-27% of extremes get set there.

## Notable asymmetry

- HOD has a stronger "last-hour" bias than LOD (24% vs 15%).
- This means **late-session rallies are more common than late-session capitulation**. End-of-day strength is a more frequent feature than end-of-day weakness on NQ.

## Trade implication

If you're hunting for the day's extreme:
- **9:30-10:00** is the most common time for both the high and the low to be set.
- **3:00-4:00 PM** is the second-most common time for the high (~24%).
- **Midday** is when extremes are LEAST likely to be set. Don't expect breakouts in the dead zone.

## How reliable?

223 days, ~11 months. Aggregate ±3pp per bucket.

## Glossary

- **HOD (High Of Day)**: the highest price reached during regular trading hours (9:30-16:00 ET).
- **LOD (Low Of Day)**: the lowest price during RTH.
- **RTH (Regular Trading Hours)**: the standard NYSE/Nasdaq session, 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET.
- **Hour bucket**: which one-hour period the new high or low was first reached in.
