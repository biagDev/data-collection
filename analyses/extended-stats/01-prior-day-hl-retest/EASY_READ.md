# Prior-Day High/Low Retest — Plain-English Table

We take yesterday's RTH session high (PDH = Previous Day High) and yesterday's RTH session low (PDL = Previous Day Low). Then we ask: how often does today's session reach back to those levels?

## Headline table

(Sample = 222 trading days; one day excluded because the very first day in the dataset has no "prior day")

| Outcome | First hour of RTH (9:30-10:30) | Full RTH session (9:30-16:00) |
|---|---|---|
| **Either PDH or PDL touched** | **172 days (77.48%)** | **204 days (91.89%)** |
| Both PDH and PDL touched (full sweep) | 4 (1.80%) | 25 (11.26%) |
| Only PDH touched | 105 (47.30%) | 106 (47.75%) |
| Only PDL touched | 63 (28.38%) | 73 (32.88%) |
| Neither touched | 50 (22.52%) | 18 (8.11%) |
| PDH touched (alone or with PDL) | 109 (49.10%) | 131 (59.01%) |
| PDL touched (alone or with PDH) | 67 (30.18%) | 98 (44.14%) |

**Plain reading:** Yesterday's high gets retested **about 49% of the time in the first hour** and **59% during the full session**. Yesterday's low gets retested less often (30% / 44%). Strong PDH-bias — about 15 percentage points more often than PDL.

## How reliable?

222 days, ~11 months. Aggregate ±2-3pp.

## Glossary

- **PDH (Previous Day High)**: the highest price of yesterday's regular trading session (9:30-16:00 ET).
- **PDL (Previous Day Low)**: yesterday's session low.
- **Sweep**: today's session retraces back to both yesterday's high AND yesterday's low.
- **RTH (Regular Trading Hours)**: 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET.
