# NQ 9:30 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 9:30 candle is the first 15-minute candle of the regular session (9:30-9:45 ET). We measured how often the price comes back to touch this candle's high or low later in the morning.

## What does "high or low gets hit" mean?

After the 9:30-9:45 candle closes, we watch the next candles. If price goes back up to the candle's high, we call that a "high hit." If price drops back to the candle's low, that's a "low hit." Just touching the level counts — even with a wick.

## Headline table

How often does the 9:30 candle's high or low get touched again later? (Sample = 232 trading days)

| Outcome | By 10:00 (next 15 min) | By 10:15 (next 30 min) |
|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **209 days (90.09%)** | **225 days (96.98%)** |
| Both sides touched (full sweep) | 14 (6.03%) | 37 (15.95%) |
| Only the high touched | 102 (43.97%) | 99 (42.67%) |
| Only the low touched | 93 (40.09%) | 89 (38.36%) |
| Neither side touched | 23 (9.91%) | 7 (3.02%) |
| High touched (alone or with low) | 116 (50.00%) | 136 (58.62%) |
| Low touched (alone or with high) | 107 (46.12%) | 126 (54.31%) |

**Plain reading:** Almost every day (97%) the price returns to touch the 9:30 candle's high or low within 45 minutes. The high gets touched slightly more often than the low (~58% vs 54%) — small bullish bias.

## How reliable are these numbers?

- 232 days = roughly 11 months of NQ history (limited by what TradingView loaded for 15-min data).
- Aggregate percentages: trustworthy to within about ±2 percentage points.
- Day-of-week percentages (n=45-47 each): trustworthy to within about ±7-10 points. Treat the leans as directional, not precise.

## Glossary

- **9:30 candle**: the 15-minute candle that opens at 9:30 AM ET (NYSE/Nasdaq open) and closes at 9:45 ET.
- **Hit / Touched**: the candle's wick (intra-bar high/low) reached the prior candle's extreme. Doesn't need to close there.
- **Sweep**: both the high and the low get touched, meaning price visited both extremes.
