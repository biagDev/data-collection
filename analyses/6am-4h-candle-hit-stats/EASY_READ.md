# NQ 6 AM ET 4-Hour Candle — Plain-English Table

The 6 AM 4-hour candle on NQ goes from 6:00 AM ET to 10:00 AM ET. This single candle covers the late pre-market hours, all the morning data drops, and the first 30 minutes of regular session.

## What's the question?

After this 4-hour candle closes (at 10:00 AM ET), how often does NQ retrace back to its high or low?

## Headline table

(Sample = 232 trading days)

| Outcome | By 10:30 (30 min after close) | By 11:00 (60 min after close) |
|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **156 days (67.24%)** | **188 days (81.03%)** |
| Both sides touched (sweep) | 4 (1.72%) | 8 (3.45%) |
| Only the high touched | 84 (36.21%) | 97 (41.81%) |
| Only the low touched | 68 (29.31%) | 83 (35.78%) |
| Neither side touched | **76 (32.76%)** | 44 (18.97%) |
| High touched (any) | 88 (37.93%) | 105 (45.26%) |
| Low touched (any) | 72 (31.03%) | 91 (39.22%) |

**Plain reading:** About **2/3 of days** retest within 30 min, **4/5 by 11:00 AM**. About 1 day in 5, the price expands away from this 4-hour range and never looks back during the first hour after close. Sweeps are very rare (~3%) because the 4-hour range is wide.

## Day-of-week breakdown

| Day | Days in sample | By 10:30 either | By 11:00 either | Bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 47 | 68.09% | 85.11% | **Strongly high-biased** (28 hi-only / 4 lo-only by 10:30) |
| Tuesday | 47 | 65.96% | 76.60% | Lo-biased (12 hi / 18 lo) |
| Wednesday | 47 | 72.34% | 85.11% | Hi-biased (19 / 15) |
| Thursday | 46 | 63.04% | 78.26% | Lo-biased (11 / 16) |
| Friday | 45 | 66.67% | 80.00% | Roughly even |

**Plain reading:** **Monday is the most lopsided day in any analysis** — after a 6 AM 4-hour candle on a Monday, the high gets retested 7x more often than the low. After the weekend, NQ drifts up to retest the 6 AM range high. Tuesday is the most resistant day overall.

## Why are these rates lower than the 15-minute candle stats?

The 4-hour candle is much **wider** than a 15-minute candle, so reaching one of its extremes requires more price travel. Compare:
- 9:30 candle (15 min wide): ~97% retest rate within 45 min
- 6 AM 4-hour candle: ~81% retest rate within 60 min

Wider range = slower to retest.

## How reliable?

232 days. Aggregate ±2pp. One day-of-week count differs by ±1 from other analyses (Thu vs Fri); affects DOW row but not aggregates.

## Glossary

- **6 AM 4-hour candle**: the 4-hour bar that opens at 6:00 AM ET and closes at 10:00 AM ET. On NQ futures, 4-hour bars typically anchor at 6 PM, 10 PM, 2 AM, 6 AM, 10 AM, 2 PM ET.
- **Sweep**: the price reaches both the high and the low after the candle closes.
