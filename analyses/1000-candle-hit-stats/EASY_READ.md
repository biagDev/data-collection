# NQ 10:00 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 10:00 candle is the third 15-minute candle of the regular session (10:00-10:15 ET). Same question as before: how often does its high or low get touched after it closes?

## Headline table

(Sample = 232 trading days)

| Outcome | By 10:30 (15 min later) | By 10:45 (30 min later) | By 11:00 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **197 days (84.91%)** | **218 (93.97%)** | **231 (99.57%)** |
| Both sides touched (full sweep) | 17 (7.33%) | 38 (16.38%) | **56 (24.14%)** |
| Only the high touched | 104 (44.83%) | 103 (44.40%) | 98 (42.24%) |
| Only the low touched | 76 (32.76%) | 77 (33.19%) | 77 (33.19%) |
| Neither side touched | 35 (15.09%) | 14 (6.03%) | **1 (0.43%)** |
| High touched (alone or with low) | 121 (52.16%) | 141 (60.78%) | 154 (66.38%) |
| Low touched (alone or with high) | 93 (40.09%) | 115 (49.57%) | 133 (57.33%) |

**Plain reading:** **Only 1 day in 232 didn't retest either side by 11:00.** This is the most reliable retest candle of the morning. Sweep rate also peaks here at 24%.

**Plain reading:** Four out of five days hit 100% retest rate by 11:00. Only Thursday had any "no-retest" days.

## How reliable are these numbers?

- 232 days, ~11 months. Aggregate ±2 points; per-day-of-week ±7-10 points.

## Glossary

- **10:00 candle**: 15-minute candle from 10:00 to 10:15 AM ET.
- **Sweep**: both the high AND low got touched.
