# Daily Patterns Vol. 1 — Plain-English Tables

Five different stats on NQ daily candles. Sample = ~6,700 daily bars (~27 years of NQ history).

---

## Stat 1: What happens after a winning or losing streak?

| Streak ending yesterday | Days in sample | Next day green | Next day red | Edge vs 50/50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 green day | 1,690 | 53.08% | 46.92% | +3.1% lean to green |
| 2 green days | 888 | 55.41% | 44.59% | +5.4% lean to green |
| 3 green days | 488 | 53.69% | 46.31% | +3.7% lean to green |
| **4 green days** | **259** | **59.85%** | **40.15%** | **+9.9% lean to green ← strongest** |
| 5+ green days | 351 | 57.26% | 42.74% | +7.3% lean to green |
| 1 red day | 1,681 | 54.49% | 45.51% | +4.5% lean to green (bounce) |
| 2 red days | 755 | 52.45% | 47.55% | +2.5% lean to green |
| 3 red days | 358 | 56.15% | 43.85% | +6.2% lean to green |
| 4 red days | 153 | 53.59% | 46.41% | +3.6% lean to green |
| **5+ red days** | **122** | **57.38%** | **42.62%** | **+7.4% lean to green** |

**Plain reading:** After bull streaks, NQ keeps going up. After bear streaks, NQ bounces. Both directions point toward green next day — that's NQ's natural upward drift showing up. The "3-up-then-down" myth is wrong.

## Stat 2: What happens after a strong-close or weak-close day?

| Yesterday's close was… | Days in sample | Today takes out yesterday's high | Today closes green |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near its high (top 25%) | 2,419 | **85.61%** | 53.82% |
| Near its low (bottom 25%) | 1,598 | (Today takes out yesterday's low) **82.48%** | 45.06% red |

**Plain reading:** A strong close (in the top 25% of the day's range) leads to today taking out yesterday's high **86% of the time**. But that doesn't mean today closes green — only 54%. The high gets tagged then often pulls back.

## Stat 3: What happens after a quiet (NR7) day?

NR7 = the smallest daily range of the last 7 days. The market just compressed.

| What happens the next day | Probability | Sample = 1,044 |
|---|---|---|
| Today's range is bigger than the NR7 day | **83.52%** |  |
| How much bigger on average | 1.68x |  |
| Today closes green | 55.94% |  |
| Today closes red | 43.39% |  |

**Plain reading:** Tight days are followed by **bigger ranges 84% of the time**. The compression releases. But which direction? About 56/43 — slight bullish lean from drift, weak directional signal.

## Stat 4: Do gaps fill?

A "gap" is when today opens at a different price than yesterday closed. "Fills" means today's price comes back to yesterday's close at some point during the day.

| Gap size in NQ points | Days in sample | Times it filled | Fill rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny (under 25 pts) | 5,884 | 5,593 | **95.05%** ← almost always fills |
| Small (25-50 pts) | 176 | 119 | **67.61%** |
| Medium (50-100 pts) | 77 | 52 | 67.53% |
| Big (100-200 pts) | 17 | 8 | 47.06% (small sample) |
| Huge (200+ pts) | 17 | 9 | 52.94% (small sample) |

**Plain reading:** Tiny gaps almost always fill (and many of those are contract-roll noise, not real gaps). Medium gaps fill ~2/3 of the time. **Big gaps fill less than half the time** — fading large gaps is a losing trade on average. The retail "always fade gaps" rule is wrong for big gaps.

## Stat 5: What happens at 20-day extremes?

| Yesterday's close was near… | Days in sample | Today closes green | Today closes red |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20-day high (top 10% of range) | 1,632 | **56.31%** | 43.69% |
| 20-day low (bottom 10% of range) | 517 | **55.51%** | 44.49% |

**Plain reading:** Both extremes lead to a green-leaning next day. **The 20-day high doesn't reverse — it usually keeps going up.** Selling 20-day highs is fighting the trend. Buying 20-day lows has a small but real bounce edge.

---

## Big-picture takeaways across all 5 stats

1. **NQ has strong upward drift.** Every conditional stat shows it. Plans that bet against this drift fight a slow, persistent headwind.

2. **Range-extension is highly predictable (~85%); direction is not (~55%).** NR7 says "expansion coming" — 84% reliable. Top-quartile close says "breakout coming" — 86% reliable. But for both, **direction is only ~55% reliable**. **Trade volatility, not direction.**

3. **Mean reversion is asymmetric.** Buying lows works modestly. Selling highs doesn't.

---

## How reliable are these numbers?

- 6,700+ daily bars is huge. Aggregate stats are trustworthy to within ±1-2 percentage points.
- Sub-samples like "after 4 green days" (n=259) are still reliable to within ±5pp.
- The smallest sub-samples (gap 100-200 pts, n=17) could be off by ±15-20pp — treat with caution.

## Glossary

- **Streak**: consecutive same-color days. "After 3 green days" means yesterday, day-before, day-before-that were all green.
- **Top quartile close**: the close was in the top 25% of the candle's range.
- **NR7**: Narrowest Range of the last 7 days. Today is the quietest day of the past week.
- **Gap fill**: today's price comes back to yesterday's closing price at some point during the session.
- **20-day range**: the highest high and lowest low of the last 20 trading days.
- **Mean reversion**: price returns toward an average level after extending away from it.
