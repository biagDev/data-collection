# GC London Session — Plain-English Table

Six 15-min candles from the london killzone on `COMEX:GC1!`. How often does gold come back to touch the high or low of each candle within the next 15-45 minutes?

(Sample = 222-223 trading days)

| Candle (ET) | Days in sample | Retest +15 | Retest +45 | Full sweep +45 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3:00** | 222 | 82.43% | 95.95% | 31.08% |
| **3:15** | 222 | 87.39% | 97.75% | 31.98% |
| **3:30** | 222 | 86.94% | 97.75% | 28.38% |
| **3:45** | 222 | 88.29% | 98.65% | 30.18% |
| **4:00** | 222 | 84.68% | 97.30% | 24.32% |
| **4:15** | 222 | 84.68% | 97.75% | 31.98% |


## Plain reading

GC's London 3:45 candle is the strongest in the session (88% retest within 15 min) but noticeably weaker than NQ/ES London 3:45 (~94%). Gold's London magnetism is less acute than equity indices. By +45 min everything converges to ~98% retest.

## How reliable?

222-223 days. Aggregate ±3pp. The 98%+ retest rates are essentially saturated — even with 5pp CI they remain high-probability events.
