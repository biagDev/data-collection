# GC 12:30 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 12:30 candle is the 15-minute candle from 12:30-12:45 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 220 trading days)

| Outcome | By 13:00 (15 min later) | By 13:15 (30 min later) | By 13:30 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **176 days (80.00%)** | **205 (93.18%)** | **212 (96.36%)** |

| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 91 (41.36%) | 121 (55.00%) | **141 (64.09%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **36.36% by 13:30** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 12:30 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (68.64% high-any vs 64.09% low-any by 13:30).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
