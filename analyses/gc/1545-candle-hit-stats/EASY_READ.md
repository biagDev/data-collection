# GC 15:45 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 15:45 candle is the 15-minute candle from 15:45-16:00 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 215 trading days)

| Outcome | By 16:15 (15 min later) | By 16:30 (30 min later) | By 16:45 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **153 days (71.16%)** | **182 (84.65%)** | **202 (93.95%)** |
| Both sides (full sweep) | 16 (7.44%) | 28 (13.02%) | **42 (19.53%)** |
| Only the high touched | 66 (30.70%) | 73 (33.95%) | 72 (33.49%) |
| Only the low touched | 71 (33.02%) | 81 (37.67%) | 88 (40.93%) |
| Neither side touched | 62 (28.84%) | 33 (15.35%) | 13 (6.05%) |
| High touched (alone or with sweep) | 82 (38.14%) | 101 (46.98%) | 114 (53.02%) |
| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 87 (40.47%) | 109 (50.70%) | **130 (60.47%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **19.53% by 16:45** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 15:45 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **low** (53.02% high-any vs 60.47% low-any by 16:45).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
