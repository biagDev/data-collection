# GC 10:15 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 10:15 candle is the 15-minute candle from 10:15-10:30 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 222 trading days)

| Outcome | By 10:45 (15 min later) | By 11:00 (30 min later) | By 11:15 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **186 days (83.78%)** | **208 (93.69%)** | **212 (95.50%)** |
| Both sides (full sweep) | 18 (8.11%) | 44 (19.82%) | **59 (26.58%)** |
| Only the high touched | 95 (42.79%) | 91 (40.99%) | 84 (37.84%) |
| Only the low touched | 73 (32.88%) | 73 (32.88%) | 69 (31.08%) |
| Neither side touched | 36 (16.22%) | 14 (6.31%) | 10 (4.50%) |
| High touched (alone or with sweep) | 113 (50.90%) | 135 (60.81%) | 143 (64.41%) |
| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 91 (40.99%) | 117 (52.70%) | **128 (57.66%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **26.58% by 11:15** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 10:15 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (64.41% high-any vs 57.66% low-any by 11:15).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
