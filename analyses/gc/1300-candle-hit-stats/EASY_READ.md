# GC 13:00 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 13:00 candle is the 15-minute candle from 13:00-13:15 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **33.79% by 14:00** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 13:00 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **low** (64.38% high-any vs 67.58% low-any by 14:00).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
