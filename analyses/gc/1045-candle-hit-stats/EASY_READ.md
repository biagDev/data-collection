# GC 10:45 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 10:45 candle is the 15-minute candle from 10:45-11:00 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 222 trading days)

| Outcome | By 11:15 (15 min later) | By 11:30 (30 min later) | By 11:45 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **198 days (89.19%)** | **210 (94.59%)** | **213 (95.95%)** |
| Both sides (full sweep) | 22 (9.91%) | 47 (21.17%) | **62 (27.93%)** |
| Only the high touched | 96 (43.24%) | 92 (41.44%) | 84 (37.84%) |
| Only the low touched | 80 (36.04%) | 71 (31.98%) | 67 (30.18%) |
| Neither side touched | 24 (10.81%) | 12 (5.41%) | 9 (4.05%) |
| High touched (alone or with sweep) | 118 (53.15%) | 139 (62.61%) | 146 (65.77%) |
| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 102 (45.95%) | 118 (53.15%) | **129 (58.11%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **27.93% by 11:45** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 10:45 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (65.77% high-any vs 58.11% low-any by 11:45).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
