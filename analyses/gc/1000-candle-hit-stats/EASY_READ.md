# GC 10:00 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 10:00 candle is the 15-minute candle from 10:00-10:15 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 222 trading days)

| Outcome | By 10:30 (15 min later) | By 10:45 (30 min later) | By 11:00 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **178 days (80.18%)** | **206 (92.79%)** | **208 (93.69%)** |
| Both sides (full sweep) | 18 (8.11%) | 43 (19.37%) | **53 (23.87%)** |
| Only the high touched | 86 (38.74%) | 89 (40.09%) | 84 (37.84%) |
| Only the low touched | 74 (33.33%) | 74 (33.33%) | 71 (31.98%) |
| Neither side touched | 44 (19.82%) | 16 (7.21%) | 14 (6.31%) |
| High touched (alone or with sweep) | 104 (46.85%) | 132 (59.46%) | 137 (61.71%) |
| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 92 (41.44%) | 117 (52.70%) | **124 (55.86%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **23.87% by 11:00** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 10:00 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (61.71% high-any vs 55.86% low-any by 11:00).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
