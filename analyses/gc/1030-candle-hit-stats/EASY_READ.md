# GC 10:30 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 10:30 candle is the 15-minute candle from 10:30-10:45 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 222 trading days)

| Outcome | By 11:00 (15 min later) | By 11:15 (30 min later) | By 11:30 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|

| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 97 (43.69%) | 124 (55.86%) | **131 (59.01%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **25.68% by 11:30** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 10:30 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (63.51% high-any vs 59.01% low-any by 11:30).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
