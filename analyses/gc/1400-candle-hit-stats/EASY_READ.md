# GC 14:00 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 14:00 candle is the 15-minute candle from 14:00-14:15 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 215 trading days)

| Outcome | By 14:30 (15 min later) | By 14:45 (30 min later) | By 15:00 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **169 days (78.60%)** | **194 (90.23%)** | **205 (95.35%)** |
| Both sides (full sweep) | 13 (6.05%) | 36 (16.74%) | **52 (24.19%)** |
| Only the high touched | 88 (40.93%) | 88 (40.93%) | 84 (39.07%) |
| Only the low touched | 68 (31.63%) | 70 (32.56%) | 69 (32.09%) |
| Neither side touched | 46 (21.40%) | 21 (9.77%) | 10 (4.65%) |
| High touched (alone or with sweep) | 101 (46.98%) | 124 (57.67%) | 136 (63.26%) |
| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 81 (37.67%) | 106 (49.30%) | **121 (56.28%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **24.19% by 15:00** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 14:00 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (63.26% high-any vs 56.28% low-any by 15:00).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
