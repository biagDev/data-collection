# GC 11:00 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 11:00 candle is the 15-minute candle from 11:00-11:15 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

(Sample = 222 trading days)

| Outcome | By 11:30 (15 min later) | By 11:45 (30 min later) | By 12:00 (45 min later) |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **28.38% by 12:00** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 11:00 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (68.92% high-any vs 55.86% low-any by 12:00).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
