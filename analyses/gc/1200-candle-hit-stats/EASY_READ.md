# GC 12:00 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 12:00 candle is the 15-minute candle from 12:00-12:15 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 222 trading days)

| Outcome | By 12:30 (15 min later) | By 12:45 (30 min later) | By 13:00 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **189 days (85.14%)** | **210 (94.59%)** | **214 (96.40%)** |

| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 107 (48.20%) | 131 (59.01%) | **140 (63.06%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **26.58% by 13:00** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 12:00 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **low** (59.91% high-any vs 63.06% low-any by 13:00).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
