# GC 15:00 Candle — Plain-English Table

The 15:00 candle is the 15-minute candle from 15:00-15:15 ET on Gold Futures (COMEX).

## Headline table

(Sample = 215 trading days)

| Outcome | By 15:30 (15 min later) | By 15:45 (30 min later) | By 16:00 (45 min later) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Either side touched** | **181 days (84.19%)** | **203 (94.42%)** | **208 (96.74%)** |
| Both sides (full sweep) | 12 (5.58%) | 33 (15.35%) | **47 (21.86%)** |
| Only the high touched | 97 (45.12%) | 95 (44.19%) | 88 (40.93%) |
| Only the low touched | 72 (33.49%) | 75 (34.88%) | 73 (33.95%) |
| Neither side touched | 34 (15.81%) | 12 (5.58%) | 7 (3.26%) |
| High touched (alone or with sweep) | 109 (50.70%) | 128 (59.53%) | 135 (62.79%) |
| Low touched (alone or with sweep) | 84 (39.07%) | 108 (50.23%) | **120 (55.81%)** |

**Plain reading:** Sweep rate reaches **21.86% by 16:00** — that's how often BOTH the high and low of the 15:00 candle get retested within 45 minutes. Bias leans **high** (62.79% high-any vs 55.81% low-any by 16:00).

## How does it compare to NQ?

NQ + ES have a clear NY 9:30 magnet (~90% retest +15 min) because that's the equity index open. Gold has no equivalent anchor at 9:30 ET — the dominant gold sessions are London (3 AM) and COMEX open (8:20 AM). Expect lower headline retest rates than NQ/ES for these NY-anchored windows; the day-of-week skew is also different.
