# NQ 9 AM ET 1-Hour Candle — Plain-English Table

The 9 AM 1-hour candle goes from 9:00 AM ET to 10:00 AM ET. It contains the final pre-market hour, the regular session open at 9:30, and the first 30 minutes of regular session.

**Plain reading:** Sits cleanly between 15-minute and 4-hour candles in retest probability. About 91% of days retest within an hour. High-bias persists (~7pp more often than low).

## How reliable?

232 days. Aggregate ±2pp.

## Glossary

- **9 AM 1-hour candle**: the 1-hour bar opening at 9:00 AM ET and closing at 10:00 AM ET.
